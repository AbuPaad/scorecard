How to Embed a YouTube Video on Your Website

A simple step-by-step tutorial

This tutorial shows how to embed a service-hosted video (a YouTube video from CNN) into your own web page. Each step includes a screenshot of where the action takes place.

Step 1 Open the video on YouTube

Go to youtube.com and search for a video. For this example we use a CNN video ("NASA's newest 'discovery machine'"). Click the video to open its watch page.

Step 2 Click the Share button

Below the video, click the Share button. A share menu will pop up with several options, including Embed.

Step 3 Click Embed

In the share menu, click Embed. A box appears showing the embed code (an <iframe>) and a Copy button.

Step 4 Copy the embed code

Click Copy to copy the <iframe> code to your clipboard. You can also select and copy it manually.

Step 5 Paste the code into your HTML file

Open your HTML file in a text editor and paste the embed code where you want the video to appear on the page. Save the file.

<iframe width="560" height="315"
    src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/GOCRs9omW1U"
    title="YouTube video player"
    frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share"
    referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Step 6 Open the page in a browser

Open your saved HTML file in a web browser. The video should now be embedded and playable on your page.
